I  ·  Our Approach

A view from the center.

Center Of The Nation is a strategy consulting firm in the northern Black Hills of South Dakota, a short drive from the geographic center of the United States. Stand at that point, 44°58′ north, 103°46′ west, and every direction lies open before you. Nothing pulls you east or west, north or south. That is where we begin every engagement.

Three hundred sixty degrees

A strategy is only as good as the view behind it. Before we recommend anything, we look in every direction: customers and competitors, employees and owners, regulators and critics, the market as it is and as it is likely to become. We are neither left nor right. We hold no house view, follow no ideology, and have no product to sell. Our only interest is the decision in front of you.

Well researched

We start with the evidence, not the conclusion. We go to primary sources, build the numbers ourselves, and test them against what could go wrong as well as what should go right. When the facts are uncertain, we say so plainly and show how much it matters. You will always know what we know, what we don't, and how we got from one to the other.

Thought out

Good advice takes time. Our work is done by senior people who think a problem through rather than run it through a template. Every recommendation comes with its reasoning laid out, so you can challenge it, defend it to your board, and act on it with confidence.

Far from the big cities

We work from the northern Black Hills, a long way from the conference rooms of New York, Boston, and Washington, and from the consensus that forms inside them. Distance brings perspective. Out here, the horizon is wide, the noise is low, and there is room to think. We meet clients wherever they are, in person or across the table on a screen, and we bring that clarity with us.

The balance point

The geographic center is the one place where all fifty states are held in balance. It is not a compromise between them. It is the point from which the whole can be seen. That is the view we offer our clients.

II  ·  Principal

Brian R. Demsey.

For decades Brian has built things from a blank page: firms, systems, and companies. An actuary by training, he measures the risk before he takes it.

Six examples show the range of the work.

An actuarial consulting firm, from scratch

He founded Brian R. Demsey & Associates and built it from nothing into a practice providing actuarial consulting and technology advice to clients in insurance, healthcare, and financial services.

Demsey, Filliger & Associates

The firm went on to provide actuarial services to more than 400 public sector entities, valuing the health and retirement benefits they had promised their employees and helping them plan to pay for them.

Partner, Ernst & Young

For years he was a partner with Ernst & Young, where he built a practice and managed a team of actuaries and executive compensation consultants.

The first benefits portal in the Fortune 100

He led the building of a unified benefits portal that gave employees one place to see and manage their benefits. It was the first of its kind among Fortune 100 companies.

RemoteNet Corporation

He founded RemoteNet to manage the computer networks of small companies remotely, giving them dependable support without an IT department of their own. Northrop Grumman also retained its services.

Hallucinations.cloud

His current venture puts the same question to eight leading AI language models at once, compares their answers, and derives the most accurate response. It is a practical answer to a troubling problem: machines that answer with complete confidence and are sometimes wrong.

Brian Demsey is the author of I Found the Bug! What AI Gets Right, What It Gets Wrong, and What We Can Do About It (BRD Press, 2026). He has served on the boards of the Beckman Laser Institute, the Children's Hospital at Mission Viejo, and the Capistrano Unified School District, and lives in Spearfish, South Dakota.

III  ·  Where We Help

The questions we take on.

We are most useful when a decision is large, the facts are uncertain, and the people around the table see it differently.

Risk and the long view

Commitments that run for decades, such as pensions, health benefits, and insurance, carry costs that are easy to promise and hard to see. We measure them, explain them plainly, and help leaders plan to meet them.

Building something new

Starting a firm, a product, or a venture from a blank page: whether to do it, how to structure it, what it will cost, and what could go wrong along the way.

Technology and artificial intelligence

Where new technology earns its place and where it does not. We help organizations adopt AI with their eyes open, knowing what it gets right, what it gets wrong, and how to check.

A second opinion

Sometimes the most valuable thing is an independent look at a plan already on the table, from someone with no stake in the answer.

IV  ·  The Name

44°58′ N 103°46′ W.

About twenty miles north of Belle Fourche, South Dakota, in a pasture in Butte County, lies the geographic center of the United States: all fifty states, from Maine to Hawaii and Alaska.

Its coordinates are 44°58′ north, 103°46′ west. Written without the symbols, they become this address: 4458N 10346W. The firm takes its name from the place.

There is no town at the center, only a survey marker, a flag, and open prairie in every direction. It is a fitting reminder of how we work: no pull toward any coast or camp, and a clear view of the whole.
